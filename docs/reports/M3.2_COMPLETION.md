- **Hold indicator:** not connected to data, because holds don't exist.

- **Manual checks not done:** screen reader, physical devices, 200% zoom, and the packet §18 walkthrough. These need a person.
- **Staff screens on a phone:** reflow is checked at 320 px, but the journey ran on desktop viewports.
- **Timing:** equal response times for hidden and missing records aren't measured.
- **Own-candidacy rule:** relies on the central authorization rule. No workspace test covers it, because a staff account can't own a candidate record.
- **Unexplained:** the earlier intermittent `color-contrast` failure didn't recur, but its cause is still unknown.
