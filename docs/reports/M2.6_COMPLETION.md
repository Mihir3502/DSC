## 1. Summary

A candidate reviews their whole application, certifies it with a checkbox and typed name, confirms, and submits. The submission is frozen, the stage moves to Application Submitted, and the candidate gets a receipt and a confirmation email. Staff see the application in a narrow queue.

## 4. Model and behavior

- **One transaction** covers the frozen record, attachment list, version status, stage change and history, candidate task completion, staff queue item, outbox row, audit event, and idempotency receipt.
- **Re-checked at commit:** ownership, stage, cycle still accepting, every section against the pinned template, the certification version, and a review token proving nothing changed since the page was read.
- **Immutable:** submissions and their attachment lists are append-only, even for the migration owner. A version becomes `SUBMITTED` only with its frozen record, and can't go back.
- **Email:** delivered after commit. A failure leaves the submission intact and is retried, up to five attempts. Outbox rows hold no address or name.
- **Audit:** version and code metadata only. A test confirms no answer or name reaches an audit row.
