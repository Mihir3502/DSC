## 2. Outcome

- Human-readable, nonsecret candidate references backed by nonsequential internal/public identifiers.
- A `candidacy` aggregate for one person pursuing one M2.1 hiring cycle/opening.
- Database-enforced prevention of more than one current candidacy for the same person and hiring cycle.

- Atomic append-only audit events, optimistic concurrency, idempotent creation, and safe retry behavior.
- Accessible candidate start and narrow staff prospect/duplicate-review screens needed for this slice.

## 5. Specification Reconciliation

Use these interpretations unless an approved current ADR says otherwise:

- A `user_account` authenticates a principal; a `person` represents the human; a `candidacy` represents that person pursuing one hiring cycle.
- One person may have multiple candidacies across different cycles over time. Never create a new person merely because the person applies again.
- M2.1 made the hiring cycle the concrete public opening. Therefore M2.2 candidacy references the immutable hiring cycle, not only the reusable position.
- Denormalized organization, position, branch, team, and published-cycle snapshot references may be retained on the candidacy only when they are database-coherent and make history/retrieval safer.
- `candidate_reference` is a human support/reference value, not an authorization credential and not proof of ownership.
- `PROSPECT` means minimal interest exists but a formal application draft has not started.
- `APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE` means a formal candidacy exists and is ready for M2.4 draft creation. M2.2 creates no `application_version`.
- Duplicate suspicion is advisory. It never silently links, rejects, or merges people solely from a score.
- An exact email match to an existing candidate-owned person is an ownership/linking decision, not permission to reveal or adopt that person.
- Candidate self-service must never reveal whether another account/person/candidacy exists.
- Staff-created prospects may be unowned until invitation/account activation, but no staff member becomes the candidate owner.
- Person merge preserves both source identities, candidacies, audit provenance, and references. It is not deletion.
- When two different active candidate accounts are attached to the two people, automated merge is prohibited; stop for an explicitly approved account-reconciliation process.
- This task may define narrow merge/review permissions if missing, but may not broaden administrator, auditor, recruiter, or HR authority beyond the accepted matrix.

Update `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` and traceability where M2.1 hiring-cycle semantics make the older position-only candidacy wording incomplete.

## 6. Domain Boundaries and Terms

### 6.1 Person

The durable canonical identity for a human. It owns general candidate contact/identity attributes but is not itself an account, application, candidacy, worker, or engagement.

### 6.2 Candidate account ownership

The authorized link allowing one verified candidate account to act for one canonical person. Ownership is checked from server-side relationships on every candidate request.

### 6.3 Candidacy

The aggregate representing one person's pursuit of one hiring cycle. It owns stage, status, assignment/ownership metadata, and stage history, but not application answers.

### 6.4 Candidate reference

A generated, bounded, human-readable reference suitable for support and staff screens. It must be nonsecret, collision safe, and independent of sequential database IDs.

### 6.5 Duplicate suspicion

A stored review case showing that two person records may represent the same human. Signals and score are internal and field-filtered; a match is never a final identity decision.

### 6.6 Staff owner

The scoped recruiter/staff user responsible for operational follow-up. `owner_user_id` never grants candidate-account ownership and never bypasses permission/scope/field policy.

## 7. Person Model

Implement `person` with equivalents of:

- Nonsequential internal ID.
- Nonsequential stable public reference when an external-safe person reference is required; do not expose it unnecessarily.
- Legal first, optional middle, and legal last name with Unicode-aware normalization and bounded display values.
- Optional preferred name.
- Candidate contact email display value plus normalized comparison value.
- Optional phone display value plus normalized E.164 comparison value when validation can establish it.
- Nullable protected date of birth only if an accepted current flow genuinely collects it; otherwise defer collection to M2.4.
- Nullable current address relationship only if needed by accepted M2.2 input; otherwise defer address creation to M2.4.
- Nullable candidate user-account ID.
- Status such as `ACTIVE`, `MERGED`, `ARCHIVED`, with explicit controlled transitions.
- Possible-duplicate indicator derived from unresolved match cases, not a freely editable boolean.
- Optimistic version and common timestamps.

Rules:

- Collect only minimal attributes required for prospect creation or candidacy ownership.
- Do not collect or store SSN/tax ID, government ID, identity documents, banking, medical, screening, employment, education, eligibility, or application answers.
- Preserve display values separately from comparison normalization when needed.
- Normalize on the server using versioned, tested rules.
- Never use names alone as a unique key.
- Never overwrite a candidate-owned email because a staff-entered prospect uses the same address.
- Candidate changes to verified identity fields are outside this task except for the already accepted account/contact behavior.
- Published URLs, logs, traces, analytics, and audit metadata must not contain names, email, phone, date of birth, or addresses.
- No ordinary hard deletion.

## 8. Candidate Account Ownership Link

### 8.1 Invariants

- One active candidate account owns at most one active canonical person.
- One active canonical person is owned by at most one active candidate account.
- A staff account, service account, or unverified candidate account cannot own a person.
- Ownership is derived from the authenticated account and database link, never a request field.
- Linking must use current account state and verified primary email evidence from M1, inside the transaction.
- A disabled/revoked account loses candidate access immediately without deleting the person or candidacies.

Enforce the one-to-one relationship with foreign keys and partial/ordinary unique constraints appropriate to the accepted account model.

### 8.2 Safe linking

Allowed paths:

1. A verified candidate with no person creates a new minimal person while starting a candidacy.
2. A verified candidate consumes a valid, unexpired, account-bound invitation that links to an unowned prospect after server-side checks.
3. An explicitly authorized, recently authenticated identity-reconciliation command links an unowned person after exact evidence and audit.

Reject:

- Client-selected person IDs.
- Linking to a person owned by another account.
- Linking based only on candidate reference, name, email claim, phone claim, or duplicate score.
- Replacing a current owner implicitly.
- Linking a staff/service account.
- Linking after invitation revocation, expiry, reuse, or scope mismatch.

## 9. Candidacy Model

Implement `candidacy` as the aggregate root with equivalents of:

- Nonsequential internal ID.
- Nonsequential external/public ID used in routes.
- Generated human-readable `candidate_reference` with a documented, collision-safe format.
- Person ID.
- Hiring-cycle ID.
- Organization, position, branch, and optional team IDs coherent with the hiring-cycle snapshot.
- Source code from a bounded catalog, such as candidate self-service or approved staff/invitation sources.
- Current stage: `PROSPECT` or `APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE` for this task.
- Candidate-visible status code mapped separately from internal stage.
- Optional staff owner user ID and/or owner team ID, each validated against current organization/scope policy.
- Proposed worker path only when selected from the hiring cycle's allowed snapshot; default `UNDECIDED` unless an accepted M2.1 handoff already fixed the choice.
- Null future classification/application references; do not create placeholder downstream rows.
- Started-at, closed-at, and closure-reason fields where applicable.
- Status: `ACTIVE`, `TERMINAL`, or `ARCHIVED` for states actually supported in this slice; do not implement `ON_HOLD` behavior early.
- Optimistic version and common timestamps.

Constraints:

- Person and hiring cycle must exist and be eligible for the requested command.
- Cycle-derived organization/position/branch/team values must be database coherent.
- At most one current/nonterminal candidacy for the same person and hiring cycle.
- Human-readable references are globally unique or namespaced with a documented collision-safe constraint.
- Staff owner must be an eligible current staff account; ownership does not confer authorization.
- Candidate routes use external IDs and always re-check the authenticated account-to-person relationship.
- No hard deletion or destructive cascade.

## 10. Stage and Status Model

Supported transitions in M2.2:

```text
create_prospect:       none -> PROSPECT
claim_prospect:    PROSPECT -> PROSPECT
start_application:    none -> APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE
start_from_prospect: PROSPECT -> APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE
close_prospect:    PROSPECT -> terminal prospect outcome
```

Rules:

- Candidate self-start normally creates `APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE` directly after every gate passes.
- Staff save-prospect creates `PROSPECT` only and may optionally trigger an accepted M1 invitation flow through an outbox/event seam.
- Starting from a prospect preserves the same candidacy and person; it does not create a duplicate record.
- Starting an application records the stage transition but creates no application template/version/answers.
- A duplicate retry returns the original safe result when the same idempotency key and canonical request are used.
- A current candidacy conflict returns a safe continue/existing outcome to its owner and a nondisclosing response to anyone else.
- A closed/cancelled/not-accepting hiring cycle cannot create or advance to `APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE`.
- Prospect closure uses bounded reason codes and does not erase person/candidacy/history.
- Withdrawal, return, submission, hold, reactivation, recruiting, classification, and later stages remain out of scope.

Candidate-visible status codes must be plain language, separately mapped, and must not expose internal duplicate review, ownership, staff notes, or risk signals.

## 12. Candidate Start-Application Flow

At `/candidate/applications/new`:

1. Require a current verified candidate session from M1.
2. Consume/validate the M2.1 handoff or a selected currently accepting hiring-cycle public reference.
3. Resolve hiring cycle, immutable published snapshot, effective acceptance window, and parent activity on the server with a trusted clock.
4. Resolve the account's current canonical person ownership.
5. If no person is linked, collect only the approved minimum identity/contact fields and create/link atomically.
6. Check current candidacy uniqueness and duplicate suspicion without revealing matched people.
7. If an owned existing candidacy exists, return a safe idempotent existing result.
8. If duplicate suspicion requires staff review under policy, create the candidacy in a safe permitted state or present an approved generic review message; never identify the match.
9. Create/transition the candidacy to `APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE`, append stage history and audit, and consume the handoff/idempotency key atomically.
10. Redirect only to an allowlisted M2.2/M2.3 continuation. Until M2.3 exists, use an approved bounded confirmation/placeholder state, not a fake portal or draft.

Revalidate the cycle at commit time. Do not trust route state, hidden inputs, client timestamps, position IDs, branch IDs, person IDs, ownership claims, or worker-path values.

## 14. Duplicate-Suspicion Service

### 14.1 Signals

Use a documented, deterministic, versioned ruleset over approved normalized values such as:

- Exact normalized verified/contact email.
- Exact normalized phone when present and validated.
- Exact normalized name plus protected date-of-birth comparison only when date of birth was lawfully collected.
- Other explicitly approved low-risk combinations.

Do not use:

- Name alone as a blocking match.
- Race, ethnicity, religion, disability, medical data, screening data, or other protected traits.
- External people-search/data-broker services.
- Unreviewed probabilistic models, embeddings, facial matching, or opaque scores.
- Plaintext restricted values in searchable indexes, logs, events, or artifacts.

### 14.2 Match record

Implement `duplicate_candidate_match` with equivalents of:

- Stable nonsequential ID.
- Canonically ordered distinct person A/B IDs so the same pair cannot be inserted in both directions.
- Ruleset/version identifier.
- Bounded signal codes, not raw email/phone/DOB/name values.
- Bounded score or confidence tier explainable from the versioned rules.
- Status: `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED_DUPLICATE`, `NOT_DUPLICATE`, or `MERGED`.
- Reviewer, reviewed-at, bounded reason code, optimistic version, and optional merge-record ID.
- Common timestamps.

One unresolved case per person pair/ruleset is enforced. Re-evaluation is deterministic and idempotent.

### 14.3 Nondisclosure

- Candidate responses say only that additional review may be needed; they never disclose match count, signal, name, email, phone, reference, owner, stage, or existence.
- Staff list/detail projections include only fields allowed by permission, assignment, scope, purpose, and recent-authentication policy.
- Unauthorized/not-found/out-of-scope duplicate cases are indistinguishable.
- Search/autocomplete does not return unrestricted person records.
- Timing, counts, badges, pagination totals, caches, logs, browser data, and error detail cannot reveal matches.

## 15. Duplicate Review and Person Merge

### 15.1 Review

Only a designated permission and permitted organization scope may:

- View a pending case through a minimal review projection.
- Mark `NOT_DUPLICATE` with a bounded reason.
- Mark `CONFIRMED_DUPLICATE` after sufficient approved evidence.
- Request/execute merge when every merge gate passes.

Review requires recent authentication when the projection contains stronger identity evidence. Candidate, ordinary recruiter, administrator support, and unassigned auditor access must not gain duplicate-review authority implicitly.

### 15.2 Merge gates

Implement merge only if all are true:

- The case is current and confirmed, both people are in the same permitted business context, and the expected versions match.
- The actor has exact merge permission, scope, recent authentication, and a bounded reason.
- The survivor is selected under deterministic documented policy or an explicit authorized choice.
- Neither person is already merged/archived incompatibly.
- At most one candidate account is linked across the pair, or both references identify the same account.
- Candidacies can be reassigned without violating same-person/same-cycle current uniqueness.
- Any conflicting current candidacies are resolved by an approved non-destructive rule; never silently discard either.
- No downstream record type present in the database lacks a reviewed reparenting/provenance strategy.

If two different candidate accounts are linked, ownership evidence conflicts, current candidacies collide without a safe rule, or a downstream reference cannot be preserved, stop the merge and create no partial changes.

### 15.3 Merge effects

In one transaction:

- Lock/version-check both people, the match case, affected candidacies, and relevant ownership rows.
- Preserve the survivor's canonical identity and safely resolve only approved fields.
- Reparent candidacies and supported references to the survivor.
- Transfer the sole candidate-account link when appropriate.
- Mark the merged person `MERGED` with an internal redirect/reference to the survivor.
- Create immutable `person_merge_record` containing IDs, safe field-resolution codes, actor/approver, reason, and time—but no duplicated raw PII.
- Mark the match `MERGED` and link the merge record.
- Append audit events and invalidate authorized projections/caches after commit.

Old candidacy references continue to resolve under their original authorization. The merged person must never become a second active identity or disappear from audit provenance.

A staff user whose own candidate person/candidacy is involved is denied business access or merge under the accepted separation rule.

## 21. Transactions, Concurrency, and Idempotency

- Person creation, ownership link, candidacy creation/transition, initial stage history, audit, and handoff consumption are one transaction.
- Staff prospect creation, duplicate case creation, stage history, audit, and optional outbox request are one transaction.
- Merge locks/version-checks the complete affected set and commits all effects/audit together.
- Use database uniqueness as the final defense against concurrent same-person/same-cycle creation.
- Use expected aggregate versions for mutable person/candidacy/match/assignment/merge commands.
- Candidate and staff create commands require scoped idempotency keys with stored canonical request fingerprint/outcome.
- Same key plus same canonical request returns the original safe outcome.
- Same key plus different request is rejected.
- Concurrent candidate starts produce one current candidacy, one initial transition, and one logical success outcome.
- Concurrent duplicate evaluations produce one unresolved pair/ruleset case.
- Concurrent merge/review/creation fails safely without orphaned links, missing history, or split ownership.
- No arbitrary sleeps; tests use barriers, controlled clocks, and independent transactions.

## 22. Database Constraints and Indexes

Migration review must include:

- Foreign keys for candidate account/person, person/candidacy, candidacy/hiring cycle and its organization/position/branch/team coherence, staff owner, stage history, duplicate pairs, and merge records.
- Unique candidate-account-to-person ownership constraints.
- Unique external IDs and human-readable candidate references.
- A partial unique constraint preventing more than one current candidacy per person/hiring cycle.
- Check constraints for non-self duplicate pairs, canonical pair ordering, allowed statuses/stages/sources/reasons, score ranges, timestamps, and status/timestamp coherence.
- One unresolved duplicate case per canonical person pair and ruleset where appropriate.
- Merge-record uniqueness preventing the same merged person from being merged twice.
- Optimistic version checks.
- `RESTRICT` defaults and no cascading delete across person/candidacy/history/match/merge/audit.
- B-tree indexes supporting exact authorized normalized contact lookup, person/hiring-cycle current candidacy, candidate-account ownership, scoped prospect retrieval, owner/stage, pending duplicate review, and reference lookup.
- No unrestricted trigram/full-text index over names/contact and no index that makes protected values broadly searchable.
- Least-privilege migration/runtime/test roles; application roles cannot update/delete append-only history/merge/audit records.

Test migration from an empty database and from the accepted M2.1 schema.

## 23. Security, Privacy, and Retention

- Classify person/contact and candidacy data as Confidential Personnel; treat date of birth and stronger identity evidence according to the accepted restricted-field policy.
- Apply minimization, purpose limitation, output encoding, CSRF protection, secure cookies, rate limits, bounded requests, safe redirects, and no-store/protected caching where appropriate.
- Candidate creation and duplicate probes must resist account/person enumeration through response text, status, redirect, timing, and rate-limit differences.

## 27. Acceptance Criteria

### AC-M2.2-01 — Canonical person

The system creates one durable, nonsequential, minimally populated canonical person independent of account/candidacy/application, with bounded normalization, status/version constraints, no prohibited identity data, and no hard delete.

### AC-M2.2-02 — Candidate ownership

Only a verified current candidate account can own one person; one person has at most one candidate owner; linking is server-derived, atomic, auditable, and rejects client selection, conflicts, expired invitations, staff/service accounts, and unverified accounts.

### AC-M2.2-03 — Candidacy integrity

A candidacy uses unique internal/public/human references, one person, one coherent M2.1 hiring-cycle snapshot, bounded source/stage/status/worker-path/owner fields, optimistic versioning, and no dangling downstream placeholders.

### AC-M2.2-04 — Current-candidacy uniqueness

Concurrent/repeated starts cannot create more than one current candidacy for the same person and hiring cycle; idempotent retries return one safe outcome and conflicting requests reject.

### AC-M2.2-05 — Stage control

Only named guarded commands create `PROSPECT` or `APPLICATION_INCOMPLETE`, advance a prospect, assign an owner, or close a prospect; append-only history and candidate-visible status remain coherent.

### AC-M2.2-06 — Accepting-cycle enforcement

Candidate/staff creation resolves current M2.1 cycle, parent activity, immutable snapshot, time window, and worker-path eligibility server side and rejects unavailable/tampered/stale openings with no partial state.

### AC-M2.2-07 — Candidate self-start

A verified candidate can start or resume only their own candidacy through a consumed safe handoff/selection, minimal identity collection, accessible UI, exact projection, and no M2.4 draft record.

### AC-M2.2-08 — Staff prospect creation

Authorized scoped staff can create a minimal unowned prospect and optional accepted invitation request; wrong role/scope/field/owner/cycle denies safely, and the staff creator never becomes candidate owner.

### AC-M2.2-09 — Duplicate suspicion

A deterministic versioned service records explainable signal codes without raw PII, canonicalizes pairs, avoids duplicate cases, uses no prohibited traits/providers/models, and never automatically merges or grants ownership.

### AC-M2.2-10 — Nondisclosure

Candidates and unauthorized staff cannot infer another person, account, candidacy, match, field, count, scope, or stage through responses, timing, URLs, caches, logs, artifacts, or direct requests.

### AC-M2.2-11 — Review and merge

Only explicitly designated, scoped, recently authenticated staff can review/merge; merge preserves both identities, candidacies, references, stage/audit history, and field-resolution provenance, while account/downstream/current-candidacy conflicts block atomically.

### AC-M2.2-12 — Authorization and projections

Every candidate/staff route and command enforces current account, audience, permission, scope, ownership, object, field, state, separation, and recent-auth rules with explicit candidate/staff/review projections.

### AC-M2.2-13 — Audit and transactions

Required person/link/candidacy/stage/owner/prospect/duplicate/merge events use safe metadata and commit atomically with business/history/outbox effects; audit failure rolls back and retries do not duplicate effects/events.

### AC-M2.2-14 — Database integrity and concurrency

Migration from empty and M2.1 succeeds with reviewed keys/checks/unique constraints/indexes/grants; deterministic races prove ownership, candidacy, match, review, and merge invariants.

### AC-M2.2-15 — Security, privacy, and accessibility

PII is minimized and absent from prohibited outputs/artifacts; normalization/enumeration/cache abuse fails safely; candidate/staff flows meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations on keyboard, screen reader, mobile, reflow, and 200% zoom.

### AC-M2.2-16 — Scope containment

Data-model/traceability updates are accurate, M0–M2.1 regressions stay green, and no application draft, portal shell, staff queue/workspace, downstream workflow, external provider, production data, generic CRUD, or M2.3+ behavior is added.
